FAQs

Straight Answers, No Guesswork

Pricing, certification, timing, and the air itself, covered below.

We own a student rental near Western. What should happen between tenants?

A full duct cleaning plus a sanitizer pass is the standard move when a lease turns over, ideally in late April once the house empties. Four years of cooking, couch, and party residue sit in the runs, and the next tenants smell whatever the last ones left every time the fan runs. We photograph the ducts too, which gives you a condition record for the property.

Can you get rid of the smell when the furnace kicks on?

Yes, though a spray can on its own would not do it. The smell lives in the film and debris lining the ductwork, and the cleaning physically carries the bulk of that out of the house. Sanitizer follows, onto bare metal, because bare metal is the only surface it earns its keep on: anything sprayed over dust just perfumes the problem and fades inside a month.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Yes, on every job, without exception. There is no government licence for duct cleaning in Ontario, so the credentials that actually mean something are certification, training, and full insurance, and we carry all three on every address we work.

Is the grey film every June actually maple seed?

A lot of it is. Old North, Woodfield, and the Byron streets sit under canopy that drops seed down and pollen for weeks, and the finer particles ride past a standard filter into the returns. If a Friday dusting is back by Sunday every June, the runs are holding a season of it, and a cleaning timed after the drop clears the backlog.

Why is our house dusty no matter what we do?

In this city it is usually return-side leaks, especially in renovated and century homes, quietly drawing dust from joist cavities and the basement into the airstream. In a flipped semi the culprit is often a boot left detached behind the new drywall, and a pressure check finds it in an afternoon instead of it finding you every dusting day.

What does a whole-home cleaning involve and how long does it take?

From your chair the visit unfolds like this. The van arrives and the technician walks the house with you, confirming the flat quote before anything comes apart. A large vacuum seals onto the trunk line and the system settles under suction. Through the morning you will hear the brush threading run after run toward the plenum, and watch the registers leave for washing one floor at a time. The blower compartment gets a full wipe while it is open, and the crew closes at the furnace with the before and after camera photos. Most detached homes here are an afternoon visit, three to four hours, longer when a finished basement tightens the access.

How often should London homes have the ducts cleaned?

Most Forest City homes sit well on a two to three year cycle. Under mature tree canopy, after a renovation, with pets in the house, or in a rental that turns over annually, the interval shortens. The camera photos from your first clean tell you what your home's real pace is.

Which areas do you cover from London?

All of London plus the surrounding Middlesex and Elgin communities, including Ilderton, Komoka, Dorchester, Strathroy, St. Thomas, and Aylmer. If you are on the edge of the map, call with your address and we will confirm coverage.

Do you serve areas outside of London, Ontario?

Yes. We serve London and nearby communities including Ilderton, Komoka, Dorchester, St. Thomas, and the surrounding area.
